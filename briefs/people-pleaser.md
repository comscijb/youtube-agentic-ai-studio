# Working title
She Said Yes to Everyone for 20 Years

# Narrative type
A

# Logline
A woman who has never refused anyone says no for the first time, and discovers that nobody knows who she is when she is not useful.

# Protagonist
Margaret Doyle, 52, school administrator in Bristol; wound: her mother loved her most when she was helpful; desire: to be needed so that she is never left.

# Other characters
- Clare, her younger sister, 47, who always has one more favour to ask
- Tom, her husband, 55, kind, but long used to being looked after

# Setting
Margaret's semi-detached house and the school office, present day, one wet spring.

# Beats
- Hook: Margaret agrees to host her sister's party the same week she agreed to cover two colleagues.
- Strategy works: every yes brings warmth, thanks and a place at the centre of everyone's life.
- Hidden cost: she cannot remember the last thing she chose for herself.
- Reversal: she says no to Clare, and nobody calls for eleven days.
- Philosophical layer: being needed is not the same as being loved.
- Ending: she sits alone in a café she chose, unsure whether she is free or abandoned.

# Tone
Warm, observant, quietly devastating; no villains.

# Must include
- the calendar on the fridge, filled in her handwriting
- the café she has walked past for years

# Must avoid
- a speech about boundaries
- the sister becoming a cartoon villain

# Target minutes
12
